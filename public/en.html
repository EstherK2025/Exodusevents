      About us
      
Technology, made simple.

      
Exodus Digital · ME-IT is the technology division of Exodus Events Agency, founded in Moncton in 2026. We support small businesses, schools and organizations that want to move forward with technology, without jargon and with security built in.

      
Our belief: security shouldn't be a luxury reserved for large companies. Every project we deliver is designed to be understood, used and protected by the people who receive it.

    

    

      
No jargonWe explain every step in plain language.

      
Security firstEvery website and computer is set up with the right protections.

      
ConfidentialityYour data, and your clients' data, stays yours.

      
Clear pricingA detailed proposal before we start, no surprises.

      
BilingualWe work in English and French.

      
LocalBased in Moncton, we stay by your side after delivery.

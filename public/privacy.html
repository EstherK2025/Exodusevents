Privacy policy.

Your trust is the foundation of our work. Here is, in plain language, what we do with your information.

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Who we are

exoduseagency.ca is operated by Exodus Events Agency, whose division Exodus Digital · ME-IT provides website, systems and cybersecurity services in Moncton (New Brunswick, Canada). For any question about your information: contact@exoduseagency.ca.

2. What we collect

Only what you choose to send us through the “Contact us” form:

  • your name and, if provided, the name of your business, school or organization;
  • your profile (business, school…) and what you need;
  • your email address and, if provided, your phone number;
  • the message you write, and the language of the form you used.

We use no advertising cookies or tracking tools. Like any website, our hosting provider may log technical data (IP address, browser type) for security and reliability purposes.

3. Why we use it

  • to answer your request and offer our services;
  • to follow up on our conversation and, if you become a client, to carry out your project.

We never sell, rent or trade your information. We never add you to a mailing list without your consent.

4. Who we share it with

To run the website, we rely on established providers that process data on our behalf: website hosting, the database where requests are stored, and the email delivery service. Some of these providers may store data outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to local laws. The website's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

5. Retention and security

Your information is kept as long as needed to handle your request and follow up, then deleted when no longer useful. Access is password-protected and limited to our team, and the website is encrypted (HTTPS).

6. Your rights

You may at any time ask to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by writing to contact@exoduseagency.ca. We reply within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
